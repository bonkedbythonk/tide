## How music reaches you

You sign in with your own Google account inside the app. Tide then asks YouTube
Music for your library, Home and search results the way the YouTube Music
website does, and plays songs with [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)
and [mpv](https://mpv.io) on your own machine. It hosts nothing and runs no
server. Lyrics come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net).

Using Tide is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service, as with any other way of
using your account. Whether and how you use it is your decision, at your own
risk. Tide keeps requests to YouTube to a modest rate, but it cannot promise
that YouTube will never limit or act on an account.
